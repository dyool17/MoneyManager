# Life events, annual budgets and bad-decision flags

Objective: explain spending spikes with life events and seasonal budgets, feed them to the AI for better projections, and flag unexplained discretionary spikes. Branch `feat/life-events`. Delivery: ask-on-risk (forecast ~900 authored lines, three units). Tests: Vitest, `cargo test`.

## Specs

- S1. Life events: "quiero un tipo de 'hitos'/'facts' que se tomen en cuenta para explicar spikes de gastos en algunas categorias, por ejemplo una mudanza, enfermedad, Paternidad, Mas dias de oficina, etc". Agreed shape: start date, optional end date, affected categories, effect kind: one-off (spike explained, excluded from the projection baseline), new normal (baseline of those categories restarts at the start date), temporary (higher spend expected while active). Stored only on this computer, never written to the phone.
- S2. AI context: "quiero que se consideren en el contexto para que la ai pueda proyectar y sugerir mejor". Events reach the assistant as a `## Life events` section; sensitive labels go only behind a sharing toggle like "Share transaction descriptions".
- S3. Annual budgets: "que considere que los presupuestos son mensuales prorrateando anualmente el gasto acumulado por ejemplo la categoria 'regalos' hay spikes en 3 meses pero la suma anualizada se prorratea en el presupuesto mensual." Per category, mode monthly (current) or annual prorated: cumulative spend compared with monthly budget × months elapsed. The app suggests annual mode for categories whose spend concentrates in few months; the user confirms.
- S4. Bad-decision classifier: "un clasificador de bad decisions para gastos que generen spikes inusuales o consuman el presupuesto de una categoria (y que no sea anormal el gasto)". Confirmed meaning ("si, asi esta bien"): flag an expense when it is big (an outlier, or it takes a large share such as ≥50% of its category budget, or pushes the category over its monthly or annualized budget), unexplained (not inside a life event for its category, not recurring, not covered by an annual budget), and discretionary (Variable group). Deterministic rule; the AI only explains and suggests. The user can dismiss a flag or turn it into a life event.
